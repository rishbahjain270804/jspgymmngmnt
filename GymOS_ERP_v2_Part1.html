1. Product Vision

Build India's most complete AI-first Gym Operating System instead of a traditional gym management application.

  • Multi-tenant SaaS
  • Mobile-first
  • AI-first workflows
  • GST + UPI + WhatsApp native
  • Cloud architecture
  • Enterprise ready

GoalDescription
Reduce Admin WorkAutomate attendance, renewals and billing.
Increase RevenueCRM, renewals and PT tracking.

ScaleSupport franchises and chains.
