2. AI Workflow Strategy

  • AI Sales Assistant follows up every lead automatically.
  • AI predicts renewal probability.
  • AI detects inactive members after configurable absence.
  • AI recommends PT upsell opportunities.
  • AI generates workouts and diet drafts for trainer review.
  • AI summarizes daily branch performance.
