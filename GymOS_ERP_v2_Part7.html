GymOS ERP v2 – Part 7

Desktop UX Specification & High-Level Wireframes

Desktop UX Principles

  • Global search on every operational screen.
  • Context-aware AI panel.
  • Sticky action bar.
  • Role-based navigation.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for high-frequency tasks.
  • Audit trail for sensitive actions.
