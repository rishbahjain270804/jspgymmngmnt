GymOS ERP v2 – Part 8

Mobile UX Specification • Member • Trainer • Reception • Owner Apps

1. Mobile UX Principles

  • Bottom navigation (maximum five tabs).
  • One-thumb operation for common tasks.
  • Offline attendance queue with automatic sync.
  • Biometric login supported.
  • Context-aware AI assistant available on every primary screen.
  • Target interaction time below two seconds.
